THE STORY BEHIND THE FINDS · DECATUR, ALABAMA

It started with
a garage full of “old junk.”

A weekend cleanout. A few forgotten treasures.
A whole new chapter.

The Rustee Relic started with a garage full of “old junk” that was supposedly going to be cleaned out one weekend.

Instead, every box told a story.

An old fishing reel that reminded someone of early mornings at the lake with their grandfather. A worn cast iron skillet that probably cooked a thousand family dinners. Rusty tools built to last longer than most things made today. We realized these weren’t just used items — they were pieces of somebody’s life, history, and hard work.

What began as sorting through forgotten treasures slowly turned into late nights researching vintage finds, cleaning up old reels at the kitchen table, and arguing over whether something was “rare” or just “really old.”

Somewhere along the way, The Rustee Relic was born.

Our goal is simple: rescue forgotten pieces of the past and get them into the hands of people who will appreciate them again. Some items are collectible, some are practical, and some are just plain cool.

Every scratch, faded label, and weathered edge has a story behind it.

We just help continue it.
